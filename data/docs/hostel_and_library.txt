Hostel Rules: Hostel gates close at 9:30 PM on weekdays and 10:30 PM on weekends. Visitors are allowed only in the common room between 4 PM and 6 PM. Hostel fee is paid once per semester.

Library Timings: The library is open from 8 AM to 8 PM Monday to Saturday. Students can borrow up to 4 books for 14 days. A fine of Rs 2 per day is charged for late returns.

Scholarship Information: Students with 85 marks or above in the previous semester and 90% attendance are eligible for the merit scholarship. Applications close on the 30th of every semester start month.
