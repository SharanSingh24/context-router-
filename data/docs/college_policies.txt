Attendance Policy: Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in every course to be eligible for the end-semester exam. Students below 75% must apply for condonation with a medical certificate.

Exam Rules: Students must carry their college ID card to the exam hall. Mobile phones and smart watches are strictly prohibited. Students arriving more than 15 minutes late will not be allowed to enter.

Leave Policy: Students can apply for up to 10 days of casual leave per semester. Medical leave requires a certificate from a registered doctor and must be submitted within 3 days of returning.
